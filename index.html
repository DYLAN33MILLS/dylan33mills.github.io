Radiographic panel positioner

Vision-guided robotics

A competition robot pairing a vision-tracking turret with a sensor-guided mecanum platform. My focus was C++ turret controls, motor iteration, and embedded integration.

03 / CU Control Systems Laboratory

Making dynamic systems behave

Modeled and controlled physical systems including a gyroscope, rotary double inverted pendulum, and ball-on-beam. Explored how controller design changes stability and response.

MATLAB / SimulinkLQRState feedback
Explore the engineering
  • Designed PID, full-state feedback, LQR, and lead-lag controllers to optimize response characteristics.
  • Modeled system dynamics in MATLAB and Simulink.
  • Evaluated stability and performance using transfer functions, root locus, Bode plots, and time-domain methods.
